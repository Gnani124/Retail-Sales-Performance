# Retail-Sales-Performance
Interactive Power BI Retail Sales Dashboard analyzing sales, orders, products, regions, stores, payment methods, and key business KPIs.


📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and monitor retail sales performance across regions, products, stores, salespersons, and payment methods.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw retail sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps management understand:

Overall sales performance
Monthly sales trends
Regional and store-level performance
Product-wise sales
Payment method performance
Order and quantity trends
Discount impact on sales
📌 Key KPIs
KPI	Value
💰 Total Sales	₹4.38M
🛒 Total Orders	2K
📦 Total Quantity Sold	16K
💵 Average Order Value	₹2.92K
🏷️ Average Discount	7.31%
📈 Dashboard Insights
Online is the highest-performing payment method by sales.
Tablet and Laptop are among the top-selling products.
Store D generated the highest sales among store locations.
Sales performance varies significantly across months and regions.
The dashboard allows users to identify high-performing products, stores, and regions.
Interactive filters make it easy to analyze sales from different business perspectives.
🔎 Business Requirements

The dashboard was designed to provide:

Sales Overview – Monitor total sales, orders, quantity and AOV.
Monthly Analysis – Identify sales trends and seasonal patterns.
Regional Analysis – Compare sales performance across regions.
Product Analysis – Identify top-performing products.
Store Analysis – Compare individual store performance.
Payment Analysis – Understand sales by payment method.
Discount Analysis – Monitor average discount levels.
Interactive Filtering – Filter the report by Region, Product, Region Manager, Salesperson and Payment Method.
